Pressure Lab

Who delivers when the match is on the line. Every ball of the IPL and full-member T20Is since 2022, scored by how much it could swing the result.

Pressure is leverage. A win-probability model trained on 815 matches scores each delivery by how far its outcome can move the result. The top fifth of deliveries by leverage are pressure balls. The map shows where leverage lives in a chase; the panels show what changes on those balls and why rules of thumb miss most of them.

Leverage by chase situation

Average Leverage Index for the batting side in the second innings. 1.0 is an average delivery; the last over of a tight chase runs above 8.

Pressure balls against ordinary balls

League-wide outcomes on the two sets. Dismissals rise, dots fall, and the swing in win probability per ball is four times larger.

Why not a rule of thumb

Share of high-leverage balls each definition captures, and the average win-probability swing on the balls it picks. A random 20% is the placebo.

Micro-pressure inside the pressure set

Three within-innings signals. None raises dismissal risk; each suppresses scoring. Batters retreat rather than gamble.

Revealed temperament. Last season's standout entry inferred psychology from the outside. This reads it from the inside: 170,000 choices made as leverage rose. Intent shift is how a player's risk appetite moves under pressure against his own normal, situation-adjusted; outcome shift is whether it pays. Together they place every player in one of five temperaments.
